About Metro Speedcom

Your Trusted Partner in Office Equipment & IT Rentals

Metro Speedcom is a leading provider of copier rentals, laptop rentals, and desktop rentals in the Philippines. We help businesses reduce operational costs through reliable, cost-efficient solutions tailored for corporations, startups, schools, and PEZA-registered companies.

We specialize in Kyocera and Samsung multifunction copier rentals with flexible plans designed to support daily operations. Our services include printing, scanning, copying, onsite technical support, and preventive maintenance to help you minimize downtime and improve productivity.

Strategically Located to Serve You

With our main office in Quezon City and a dedicated warehouse in Concepcion, Tarlac, we efficiently serve clients across Metro Manila, Central Luzon, and nearby provinces with fast delivery and responsive service support.
